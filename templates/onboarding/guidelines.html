1. Objective Multi-Axis Ranking & Anti-Bias Standards

Evaluating pairwise model responses (Response A vs Response B) requires rigorous adherence to multi-dimensional rubrics:

  • Zero Length Bias: Never prefer a completion simply because it is verbose or filled with decorative bullet points. Factual accuracy and conciseness outweigh fluff.
  • Sycophancy Penalty: Penalize models that falsely agree with user misconceptions rather than providing objective ground truth.
  • Mandatory Rationales: Every preference ranking must include a detailed comparative justification citing specific paragraphs.

1. Strict Verbatim & Acoustic Tagging Conventions

Speech recognition models rely on exact acoustic-phonetic alignment between audio and transcript text:

  • Verbatim Fidelity: Capture every spoken word, stutter, filler ('um', 'uh'), and false start without editing for grammar.
  • Acoustic Event Tags: Accurately insert standard bracketed tags ([laughter], [applause], [inaudible 00:03], [crosstalk]).
  • Code-Switching Boundaries: Transcribe multi-language transitions exactly as spoken in African dialects.

2. Authentic Native Orthography & Special Characters

African languages must be written with authentic phonetics and proper orthography:

Hausa Hooked Consonants: ɓ (Ɓ), ɗ (Ɗ), ƙ (Ƙ), ƴ (Ƴ)
Yoruba & Igbo Tone Accents: à, á, è, é, ọ, ẹ, ụ, ị, ń

Always use the on-screen special character toolbar inside the task workspace rather than substituting standard Latin letters (e.g. do not write 'kudin' when the word is 'kuɗin').
