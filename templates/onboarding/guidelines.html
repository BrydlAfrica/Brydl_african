Brydl AI Quality Standards & Honor Code

Mandatory Passing Requirement: You must carefully read these 4 core operational principles. You will take an 8-question gating quiz and must achieve a 100% pass score to unlock the Stage 3 qualification test.
{# Rule 1 #}

1. Zero-Tolerance AI & Machine-Translation Rule

Brydl AI produces human-aligned evaluation data for frontier AI labs. Using Google Translate, DeepL, ChatGPT, or any automated tool to translate or generate responses is strictly prohibited.

  • Submissions containing machine translation artifacts, unnatural English calques, or AI watermarks are flagged automatically.
  • Violations trigger immediate account disqualification, forfeiture of pending payouts, and permanent blacklisting across our platform.
{# Rule 2 #}

2. Authentic Native Orthography & Special Characters

African languages must be written with authentic phonetics and proper orthography:

Hausa Hooked Consonants: ɓ (Ɓ), ɗ (Ɗ), ƙ (Ƙ), ƴ (Ƴ)
Yoruba & Igbo Tone Accents: à, á, è, é, ọ, ẹ, ụ, ị, ń

Always use the on-screen special character toolbar inside the task workspace rather than substituting standard Latin letters (e.g. do not write 'kudin' when the word is 'kuɗin').

{# Rule 3 #}

3. Absolute Client Confidentiality & NDA

All prompts, AI model outputs, benchmark evaluation questions, and client names are strictly confidential trade secrets.

  • Never take screenshots or post task content on WhatsApp, Telegram, Twitter, or Discord.
  • All annotators execute a legally binding digital Non-Disclosure Agreement (NDA) in Stage 7 before active production work.
{# Rule 4 #}

4. Multi-Dimensional Rubric Scoring & Continuous Quality

When evaluating AI outputs, you must score each response objectively across multiple dimensions (Helpfulness, Harmlessness, Factuality, Cultural Authenticity) on standard 1–5 or 1–7 continuous sliders.

Submissions undergo blind dual-review and continuous gold-standard calibration. High-quality annotators receive higher task limits, priority pod assignments, and bonus reward rates.
