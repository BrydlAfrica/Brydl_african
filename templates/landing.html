Frequently Asked Questions

Factual information for enterprise clients, AI safety researchers, and annotators.

What is Brydl?

Brydl (Brydl AI) is an enterprise-grade African Language AI Safety, Adversarial Red-Teaming, and Model Alignment Operating System developed by Gretek Technologies. It protects frontier artificial intelligence models and speech systems from cultural exploits, jailbreaks, and dialect failures across major African languages.

What does Brydl do?

Brydl stress-tests large language models (LLMs) and voice agents through live adversarial red-teaming (RedLab v14 OS), collects phoneme-aligned paired speech corpora across noisy African acoustic environments, conducts cultural human-in-the-loop preference alignment (RLHF/DPO), and generates verified NDPA compliance logs.

Who is Brydl designed for?

Brydl is built for global foundation AI labs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Meta, Mistral), enterprise technology firms, African fintechs and telecoms deploying voice/chat AI, as well as vetted native African linguists, security researchers, annotators, and compliance auditors.

What problems does Brydl solve?

Standard AI guardrails fail under low-resource language attacks (Hausa, Yoruba, Igbo, Swahili, Amharic, Pidgin), Western alignment bias misinterprets local legal/cultural proverbs, and speech models break under heavy accents. Brydl resolves these failures while ensuring exact Decimal compensation for African digital workers.

Why should someone choose Brydl?

Unlike generic crowd-working platforms, Brydl combines native African cultural grounding, a battle-tested dual-engine architecture (Flask + FastAPI RedLab), Zero Data Retention with AES-128 MultiFernet encryption, strict SSRF/IDOR protections, and instant direct Nigerian bank payouts.

Where and how can someone access Brydl?

Brydl is accessible online through its secure web portal. Enterprise clients can request custom safety benchmarks and corpora, while native linguists and security researchers can sign in to participate in verified annotation and red-teaming campaigns.
