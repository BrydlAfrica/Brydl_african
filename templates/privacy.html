Data Protection & Privacy Notice

Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) principles — how Brydl collects, uses, protects and retains personal data. Last updated: September 2026.

1. Who we are

Brydl is an African-language AI evaluation and safety platform developed by Gretek Technologies, based in Kano, Nigeria. For the personal data of our contributors, researchers, and website visitors, Brydl is the data controller. For the source model prompts and evaluation datasets our clients upload for testing, the client remains the data controller and Brydl acts as the data processor, handling that data strictly on the client's documented instructions.

Data protection contact: the Data Protection focal point, reachable through your Brydl team contact or at our Kano office address.

2. Data we collect

  • Worker account data: full name, email address, role, password (stored only as a modern salted hash), theme preference, and terms-acceptance records.
  • Payout data: bank name, bank account type, and bank account number — the account number is encrypted at rest with an industry-standard cipher and only ever decrypted inside a payout process by authorised staff.
  • Work records: task submissions, quality scores, reviewer notes, consensus verdicts and justification texts — this is the work product you produce on the platform.
  • Security records: sign-in audit events (email, IP address, timestamp, action) kept in an append-only trail.
  • Client project data: project definitions, uploaded source data and export deliveries.

We do not collect advertising identifiers, and we run no third-party advertising or tracking scripts on this platform.

3. Why we use it (lawful basis)

  • Contract: running your account, assigning work, reviewing submissions and paying you exactly what you earned.
  • Legal obligation: payroll, tax and accounting records; audit trails required of a financial platform.
  • Legitimate interests: platform security (login lockouts, session revocation, fraud prevention) and service integrity.
  • Client instructions: labelling work performed on client source data is processed purely to deliver the contracted dataset.

4. Sharing

Personal data is shared only when the job requires it:

  • Payout providers (our bank or our licensed payment processor) receive the bank details needed to send your money — nothing more.
  • Clients receive work product (labels, scores, justifications) for the projects they commissioned — never your bank details or password.
  • Nobody else. We sell no data, and we run no ad trackers.

5. Security

Bank numbers encrypted at rest; passwords hashed, never stored in readable form; HTTPS enforced in production; per-role access control (annotators never see gold answers or payout data); every administrative action written to an append-only audit trail; regular credential rotation and verified off-site backups. Workers are issued individual accounts so all actions are attributable.

6. Retention

  • Account and payout records: retained for as long as your account is active, then as long as Nigerian payroll, tax and accounting rules require.
  • Work product: retained per the client's project contract so quality can be audited and datasets re-delivered.
  • Security logs: retained in the append-only audit trail for platform integrity investigations.

7. Your rights

You may request: access to your personal data; correction of inaccurate data; deletion of data we no longer need (subject to payroll/audit retention); and restriction of processing while a dispute is resolved. Requests are free, answered within a reasonable time, and verifiable — we will confirm your identity before acting.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
