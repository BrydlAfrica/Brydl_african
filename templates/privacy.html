Data Protection (NDPR & NDPA 2023)

BRYDL CONTRIBUTOR PRIVACY NOTICE

Operating country: Nigeria • Contact: inquiry@brydldata.com.ng / privacy@brydldata.com.ng

Version 1.1
Effective: November 2026
{# ---- Section 1 ---- #}

1. About This Notice & Data Controller Details (NDPA Section 27)

This Privacy Notice is issued pursuant to Section 27 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA) and NDPR principles. It explains how personal data relating to applicants, registered contributors and other individuals interacting with the Brydl contributor platform may be collected, used, stored, shared and protected.

Platform and Data Controller Information:
  • • Platform & Brand: Brydl Platform (operating under Gretek Technologies, an unincorporated technology initiative)
  • • Data Controllers: The founding and operational leadership team of Gretek Technologies / Brydl (acting as joint data controllers under Section 65 of the NDPA)
  • • Operating locations: Kano (Evaluator & Linguist Operations) and Abuja (Administrative & Regulatory Coordination), Nigeria
  • • Privacy focal contacts: privacy@brydldata.com.ng • inquiry@brydldata.com.ng
{# ---- Section 2 ---- #}

2. Principles of Personal Data Processing (NDPA Section 24)

Personal data is processed in strict compliance with the core principles established under Section 24 of the NDPA: processed lawfully, fairly, and in a transparent manner; collected for specified, explicit, and legitimate purposes; adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary; accurate and kept up to date; retained no longer than necessary; and secured with appropriate technical and organisational safeguards.

{# ---- Section 3 ---- #}

3. Personal Data We May Collect

Depending on the features used and projects in which you participate, we may collect:

  • 3.1 Account and contact information: Name, username, email address, telephone number, authentication records, profile details, and communication preferences.
  • 3.2 Eligibility and qualification information: Age/eligibility confirmation, language proficiency and assessment scores, educational/skills background, calibration results, and identity verification records where required.
  • 3.3 Project and work information: Task submissions, annotations, evaluation responses, classifications, reviewer feedback, quality scores, revision history, and task timestamps.
  • 3.4 Payment information: Bank name, account name, and bank account number (encrypted at rest), payout status, transaction references, and payment correspondence. Banking PINs, passwords, and OTPs are never requested.
  • 3.5 Technical and security information: IP address, access logs, device/browser details, session authentication logs, and security event trails.
  • 3.6 Support and complaint information: Messages sent to support, complaints, appeals, and records of dispute determinations.
  • 3.7 Sensitive or special-category information: Certain safety evaluation projects may involve sensitive prompt content. Contributors are not asked to disclose personal sensitive data about themselves unless lawfully justified.
{# ---- Section 4 ---- #}

4. Why We Process Personal Data

Personal data is processed strictly to:

  • Create and administer contributor accounts.
  • Assess eligibility and language or task qualifications.
  • Match contributors to relevant project requirements.
  • Assign, receive and review project work.
  • Communicate instructions, revisions and project updates.
  • Calculate and process approved compensation.
  • Prevent fraud, account abuse and unauthorised access.
  • Investigate complaints, appeals and security incidents.
  • Maintain records required for accounting, contractual or legal purposes.
  • Improve the quality, reliability and security of the Platform.
  • Meet legal and regulatory obligations under Nigerian law.
{# ---- Section 5 ---- #}

5. Lawful Bases for Processing (NDPA Section 25)

Under Section 25 of the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA), processing is conducted strictly under recognised statutory lawful bases:

  • Section 25(1)(b)(i) Contractual Necessity: Performing contributor agreements, assigning tasks, evaluating submissions, and processing approved compensation to your registered bank account under the Contributor Terms & Conditions.
  • Section 25(1)(b)(ii) Legal Obligation: Compliance with applicable Personal Income Tax Act (PITA) / State Internal Revenue Service withholding rules, AML/CBN financial record-keeping for NUBAN transfers, and NDPA compliance audit trails.
  • Section 25(1)(b)(v) Legitimate Interests: Protecting platform integrity, verifying quality rubrics, preventing multi-account abuse/fraud, and maintaining security audit logging (balanced against contributor rights).
  • Section 25(1)(a) & Section 26 Consent: Where specific secondary processing relies on voluntary consent (such as optional platform updates and newsletter notifications). Section 26 governs the conditions, proof, clear withdrawal mechanisms, and affirmative unbundled nature of such consent.
{# ---- Section 6 ---- #}

6. How We Share Personal Data

Personal data is shared only with authorised project clients (anonymised deliverable outputs), authorised reviewers/administrators, hosting/infrastructure providers, and licensed Nigerian payment processors (Paystack/banking institutions) involved in processing compensation. Contributor personal identities are severed from public deliverables.

{# ---- Section 7 ---- #}

7. Cross-Border Transfers (NDPA Sections 41–43)

Platform cloud infrastructure is hosted on secure servers located in Frankfurt, Germany (EU) and the United States (Render Services, Inc. and Supabase / AWS). Cross-border transfers to these cloud infrastructure providers are governed by Data Processing Addenda (DPAs) and Standard Contractual Clauses under NDPA Sections 41–42, and are necessary for the performance of the contributor contract under Section 43(1)(b).

{# ---- Section 8 ---- #}

8. Data Retention Periods & Justifications

Personal data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill its processing purpose and statutory obligations:

  • Account & Profile Details: Retained for active account duration + 12 months post-closure for contractual dispute limitation compliance.
  • Payment & Financial Records: Retained for 7 years under Nigerian financial and tax audit standards (PITA / SIRS rules).
  • Consent Records & Legal Evidence: Retained for 7 years as statutory proof of consent under NDPA Section 26(1).
  • IP Addresses & Security Logs: Retained for 24 months for threat intelligence, cybersecurity investigation, and audit trail integrity under NDPA Section 39.
  • Task Submissions & Work Product: Retained for project lifecycle + 3 years as client benchmark assets with personal identifiers severed.
  • Security Backups: System backups rotated on a scheduled 30–90 day cycle with encrypted storage.
{# ---- Section 9 ---- #}

9. Security, Integrity and Confidentiality (NDPA Section 39)

In accordance with Section 39 of the NDPA, technical and organisational safeguards include role-based access controls, scrypt password hashing, AES cipher-based encryption at rest for bank details, HTTPS / TLS 1.3 transport encryption, single-hop proxy filtering, and append-only security audit logging.

{# ---- Section 10 ---- #}

10. Your rights and NDPA Protections (Sections 34–38)

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (Sections 34–38) and NDPR principles, your statutory rights are supported through the following workflows:

Self-Service Workflows:

  • Profile Review & Confirmation (Section 34): View account information directly on Profile.
  • Consent History Log (Section 34): Inspect your durable consent history table at Acceptance History.
  • Work & Earnings History (Section 34): Review tasks, QC scores, and payouts on Task History.
  • Data Portability (Section 38): Export personal submission history via structured CSV download on the History view.
  • Withdrawal of Optional Consent (Section 35): Toggle optional email preferences on the Profile settings view at any time with zero impact on task access.

Manual Request Workflows (via privacy@brydldata.com.ng):

  • Full Subject Access Requests (SARs) (Section 34): Request comprehensive security and audit logs.
  • Rectification of Identity Data (Section 34): Request correction of locked fields (e.g. registered email or bank account mismatch).
  • Account Erasure & Restriction (Sections 34 & 38): Request erasure or restriction of processing under contested circumstances (subject to statutory tax retention).
  • Right to Object (Section 36): Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Automated Decision-Making Safeguards (Section 37): Right not to be subject to decisions based solely on automated profiling or automated QA scoring without human auditor arbitration.
  • Regulatory Complaint: Lodge a complaint with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

Send manual privacy requests to: privacy@brydldata.com.ng / inquiry@brydldata.com.ng.

{# ---- Sections 11-14 ---- #}

11. Cookies and Technical Storage

The Platform uses essential session cookies for authentication, CSRF security tokens, and theme preferences. No third-party advertising or cross-site tracking cookies are deployed.

12. Children's Data

The contributor platform is strictly intended for adults aged 18 and above. The Platform does not knowingly collect personal data from minors.

13. Changes to This Notice

Updates to this Privacy Notice are posted with a revised version number and publication date.

14. Contact and Inquiries

For privacy requests, questions, or complaints:
Email: privacy@brydldata.com.ng / inquiry@brydldata.com.ng • Platform: Brydl
