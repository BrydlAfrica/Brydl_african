{# Clause 1 #}

1. Introduction and Contracting Party

These Contributor Terms & Conditions (“Terms”) govern participation in projects and activities made available through the Brydl platform (“Brydl”, “Platform”, “we”, “us” or “our”). Brydl is a platform for African-language AI evaluation, safety research, red-teaming, language data work and related human evaluation activities operating in Nigeria. Official inquiries and notices may be directed to inquiry@brydldata.com.ng.

By creating a contributor account, accepting a project, or submitting work through the Platform, you agree to these Terms and project-specific requirements.

{# Clause 2 #}

2. Eligibility and Account Registration

Contributors must be at least 18 years old unless expressly permitted by a project and applicable law. You must provide accurate registration details, maintain credential confidentiality, avoid multi-account evasion, and not impersonate others. Identity verification is processed in accordance with the Brydl Privacy Notice.

{# Clause 3 #}

3. Nature of the Contributor Relationship

Contributors participate as independent project contributors. Participation does not create an employment relationship, partnership, agency, or joint venture. Contributors are not guaranteed minimum tasks, hours, or earnings, and may accept or decline project opportunities freely. Contributors are responsible for their own personal tax obligations under Nigerian law.

{# Clause 4 #}

4. Projects and Task Requirements

Projects include African-language AI evaluation, translation, RLHF preference ranking, red-teaming, speech data collection, and safety auditing. Contributors must follow supplied instructions, never fabricate answers or copy work, disclose conflicts of interest, and avoid prohibited automated generative AI tools where disallowed.

{# Clause 5 #}

5. Quality Assurance, Review and Revisions

Submissions are reviewed by human reviewers, project administrators, or automated checks for accuracy, completeness, and adherence to guidelines. Materially failing submissions may be returned for revision or rejected with explanation. Good-faith disagreements over language interpretation do not constitute misconduct.

{# Clause 6 #}

6. Compensation and Payments

Compensation is calculated per approved task or milestone as disclosed prior to work. Rates and ledger balances are tracked in USD and disbursed in Nigerian Naira (NGN). No application or registration fees are ever charged. Unearned payouts for fraudulent submissions may be withheld, but undisputed approved earnings are honored.

{# Clause 7 #}

7. Confidentiality and Information Security

Project data, prompts, model outputs, benchmark rubrics, and client identities are confidential trade secrets. Contributors must not copy, screenshot, publish, or distribute project materials. Confidentiality obligations survive account closure.

{# Clause 8 #}

8. Personal Data and Privacy

Personal data is processed in accordance with the Brydl Privacy Notice and NDPA 2023 principles. Contributors must not upload third-party personal data without authorization and must report data security incidents promptly.

{# Clause 9 #}

9. Intellectual Property and Deliverables

Upon full payment of approved compensation, rights in submitted Deliverables are assigned or licensed to the contracting party and its designated research clients. Contributors retain rights in their independently developed pre-existing materials.

{# Clause 10 #}

10. Sensitive Content and Contributor Wellbeing

Certain AI safety evaluations involve sensitive or offensive language. Projects disclose potential content categories before task acceptance, and contributors may decline tasks without penalty.

{# Clause 11 #}

11. Prohibited Conduct

Fabrication, plagiarism, score manipulation, credential sharing, unauthorized data scraping, harassment, malware distribution, and automated bots are strictly prohibited and result in immediate account deactivation.

{# Clauses 12 to 18 #}

12. Account Restrictions, Suspension and Termination

Accounts may be suspended for investigation of security or policy breaches. Approved undisputed compensation remains payable.

13. Platform Availability and Opportunities

Brydl does not guarantee uninterrupted service or continuous project volume. Project allocation depends on client demand and quality records.

14. Liability

Liability is governed by the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Mandatory statutory rights cannot be excluded.

15. Changes to These Terms

Material changes prompt contributors to accept updated versions. Historical consent records are permanently preserved.

16. Complaints and Dispute Resolution

Complaints regarding reviews, payments, or access are handled via inquiry@brydldata.com.ng. Governed by Nigerian law.

17. General Provisions & 18. Contact

Severability, non-waiver, and assignment rules apply. Support: inquiry@brydldata.com.ng.
