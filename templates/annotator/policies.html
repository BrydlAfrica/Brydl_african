Brydl Contributor Policies

Policy 1

Contributor Code of Conduct

  1. Treat other contributors, reviewers, clients and Platform staff respectfully.
  2. Submit honest, original and good-faith work.
  3. Follow project instructions and disclose relevant limitations.
  4. Respect language, cultural and contextual differences.
  5. Avoid harassment, threats, discrimination and intimidation.
  6. Protect confidential information and personal data.
  7. Avoid conflicts of interest or disclose them promptly.
  8. Report suspected misconduct, fraud or security issues.
  9. Avoid misrepresenting qualifications, identity or experience.
  10. Use the Platform only for authorised activities.
{# ---- Policy 2 ---- #}
Policy 2

Work Quality and Review Policy

  1. Every project provides clear task instructions and acceptance criteria before work begins.
  2. Contributors complete work independently unless collaboration is expressly permitted.
  3. Reviewers assess submissions against stated criteria rather than personal preference.
  4. A rejection includes a reason or relevant quality category where reasonably practicable.
  5. Contributors have a way to request clarification or appeal through inquiry@brydldata.com.ng.
  6. Reviewers must not falsify scores, retaliate against contributors or manipulate acceptance decisions.
  7. Disagreement about language interpretation is recorded where relevant, not automatically treated as poor performance.
  8. Project owners monitor reviewer consistency and escalate recurring disagreements.
  9. Any gold-standard or reference answer is protected from unauthorised disclosure.
  10. Quality thresholds and material changes must be communicated before they affect contributor compensation.
{# ---- Policy 3 ---- #}
Policy 3

Compensation and Payment Policy

  1. The compensation basis and rate are disclosed before task acceptance.
  2. Contributors are informed of relevant payment conditions, schedules, thresholds and transaction charges.
  3. Approved work is recorded accurately in the earnings ledger.
  4. Payment status distinguishes at least: pending review, approved, payment processing, paid, disputed and rejected.
  5. A disputed task does not place all unrelated approved earnings on hold.
  6. Payment holds have a documented reason and are limited to what is reasonably necessary.
  7. Contributors have a support route for payment inquiries.
  8. The Platform does not advertise payment methods or timelines that have not been verified in production.
  9. Contributors are never asked for a banking PIN, password or OTP.
  10. No guaranteed task volume or earnings are promised.
{# ---- Policy 4 ---- #}
Policy 4

Confidentiality and Data Handling Policy

  1. Project information must be used only for the assigned purpose.
  2. Contributors must not publish or share task content, prompts, model responses, datasets or client materials.
  3. Personal data must not be copied into unauthorised tools or external services.
  4. Contributors must use secure devices and protect account credentials.
  5. Contributors must not attempt to identify anonymised individuals or re-identify datasets.
  6. Suspected data exposure must be reported promptly.
  7. Project files must be deleted or returned when instructed.
  8. Access is limited to personnel authorised for the relevant project.
  9. Administrators and reviewers follow strict confidentiality obligations.
  10. Any project involving sensitive or special-category data requires additional documented safeguards.
{# ---- Policy 5 ---- #}
Policy 5

Sensitive Content and Wellbeing Policy

  1. Projects involving potentially disturbing material disclose the nature of the content before acceptance.
  2. Contributors may decline a project before beginning it.
  3. Contributors should follow project-specific exposure limits and handling instructions.
  4. Contributors may report content that falls outside the stated project scope.
  5. Reviewers must not penalise a contributor merely for raising a good-faith wellbeing concern.
  6. Any pause or reassignment is offered only where operationally available.
  7. The Platform does not promise counselling or clinical support unless such a service has been arranged.
{# ---- Policy 6 ---- #}
Policy 6

Complaints and Appeals Policy

  1. Contact channel: inquiry@brydldata.com.ng.
  2. Complaints record the date, category and relevant project reference.
  3. Receipt is acknowledged within a reasonable operational timeframe.
  4. Issues are assigned to an appropriate reviewer who is not directly responsible for the disputed decision where practicable.
  5. Relevant evidence is reviewed and a reasoned outcome is provided.
  6. Complainants are protected from retaliation for raising good-faith concerns.
  7. Complaint records are kept only for as long as necessary and lawfully permitted.
{# ---- Policy 7 ---- #}
Policy 7

Account Security and Acceptable Use Policy

Contributors must not:

  • Share account credentials or impersonate another person.
  • Attempt to bypass access controls or exploit software vulnerabilities.
  • Upload malware, malicious files, or scrape data without authorisation.
  • Manipulate task assignment, qualification, or payment processes.
  • Use another contributor's account or submit work on their behalf without permission.
{# ---- Policy 8 ---- #}
Policy 8

Policy Administration

The Platform displays current policy versions, retains historical records, trains authorised staff, applies policies consistently, and documents enforcement decisions.
