These Contributor Terms & Conditions (“Terms”) govern participation in projects and activities made available through the Brydl platform (“Brydl”, “Platform”, “we”, “us” or “our”).

Brydl is a platform for African-language AI evaluation, safety research, red-teaming, language data work and related human evaluation activities operating in the Federal Republic of Nigeria. Official inquiries and notices may be directed to inquiry@brydldata.com.ng.

By creating a contributor account, accepting a project, or submitting work through the Platform, you agree to these Terms and project-specific requirements. If you do not agree, do not accept tasks or submit work through the Platform.

  • Other related research or data tasks.

  • 5.1. Submitted work may be reviewed by authorised human reviewers, project administrators or quality-assurance processes.

  • 5.3. Work may be returned for correction, clarification or revision where the project permits it.
  • 5.4. Work may be rejected where it materially fails the stated requirements. Where reasonably practicable, the contributor should receive an explanation of the relevant issue.

  • 5.6. Disagreement with a reviewer, a language interpretation or a research conclusion does not by itself constitute misconduct.
  • 5.7. Review decisions should be made in good faith and should not be manipulated to avoid paying for work that has already met the agreed requirements.

11. Prohibited Conduct

Contributors must not:

  • Submit fabricated, plagiarised or deliberately misleading work.
  • Manipulate qualification tests, quality scores or payment records.
  • Use another person's account or identity.
  • Share confidential project materials without permission.
  • Attempt unauthorised access to Platform systems or other accounts.
  • Harass, threaten, discriminate against or intimidate other contributors or staff.
  • Bribe, collude with or improperly influence reviewers.

  • Circumvent project restrictions or use prohibited automation.
